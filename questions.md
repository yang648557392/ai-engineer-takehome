# Required evaluation questions

Your system must handle these 7.
Write your own expected answers, then add at least 3 questions of your own.

1. What was Kettlebridge ARR at the end of Q2 2027?
2. How quickly must a customer deletion request be executed, and what happens to embeddings derived from that customer's data?
3. Why does Orlo not have a namespace on the shared vector cluster, and what has to happen before it can get one?
4. If a venture hits 100% of its monthly agent gateway spend cap, which requests still get served? Answer for every venture.
5. What is Orlo's monthly user churn rate?
6. What is the retention window for embeddings derived from customer data?
7. If the Pindrop pruning job lands and delivers the expected reduction, roughly how many vectors will the shared cluster hold?
