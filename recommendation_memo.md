# Recommendation: Start with Kettlebridge

**To:** Group Operations Lead  
**From:** AI Engineering

I recommend a small Kettlebridge pilot. Do not launch the tool across all four ventures yet. Keep the pilot read-only and easy to stop.

## Cost

The estimated cost is EUR 62 per 1,000 answers [D018]. Therefore, 10,000 answers would cost about EUR 620. This is about 21% of Kettlebridge’s monthly AI budget [D010]. The pilot needs a spending limit and cost alerts.

## What Must Happen First

Vantel requires at least 30 test cases, but this project has only 10 [D012].

Pindrop must delete 1.08 million old vectors [D024]. Orlo must complete its data protection review and receive DPO approval [D007][D023]. The platform team must also finish the open gateway controls [D011].

## Why Kettlebridge?

Kettlebridge already has a production vector namespace [D006]. Its product rules require citations and refusal when information is missing [D017]. It also has 120 human-written summaries for testing [D018].

## Main Risk

All ventures use the same platform. One past incident affected three ventures, and about 40% of Kettlebridge’s requests timed out [D011]. A shared system can spread one failure to several companies.
