# Incident Report 2027-07-30 - Gateway spend cap misfire

## Impact

For roughly three hours Marrow & Co received HTTP 429 from the agent gateway on all routes.
No customer facing impact; Marrow uses the gateway for internal drafting only.

## Cause

The Q3 spend cap sheet was loaded with the Q2 sheet still active, and the gateway summed both.
Marrow's effective cap was read as EUR 1,400 with EUR 1,410 already spent.

## Resolution

The Q2 sheet was retired from the gateway configuration.
Marrow's correct Q3 cap is the figure in the Q3 sheet.

## Actions

- Gateway to reject a second active cap sheet for the same venture. Owner platform team. Open.
