# People - Levelling Framework v1

Published as promised in the January retention note.

## Levels

Five levels for individual contributors, L1 to L5, and three for managers, M1 to M3.
Each level has a written description of scope, autonomy and impact.

## Rollout

All ventures map existing roles to levels by end of Q3 2027.
Compensation bands per level are published separately by group finance.

This framework covers people and roles.
It does not cover data classification levels or confidentiality tiers.
