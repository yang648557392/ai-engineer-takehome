# People Note - Employee Retention 2026

Group wide voluntary attrition in 2026 was 11%, down from 17% in 2025.
Retention was strongest in the platform team, where nobody left voluntarily.
Retention was weakest at Marrow & Co, where three of eleven people left.

Exit interviews cite career path clarity as the main reason for leaving.
The people team will publish a levelling framework in Q2 2027.

This note concerns employee retention only.
It has nothing to do with data retention windows on platform infrastructure.
