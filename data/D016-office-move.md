# Facilities - Rotterdam Office Move

Marrow & Co moves to a new Rotterdam office on 1 October 2027.
The new space seats 18 and has two meeting rooms.
Parking is not included and staff should use the public garage two streets away.

Desk booking will continue through the existing tool.
Questions go to the facilities mailbox.
