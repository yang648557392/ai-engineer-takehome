# Orlo - Product Metrics, July 2027

## Usage

- Monthly active users: 41,200
- Daily active users: 9,800
- Identifications per day: 27,000

## Retention

- Day 1 retention: 61%
- Day 7 retention: 44%
- Day 30 retention: 38%

Retention here means the share of new users still opening the app after the stated number of days.
It is a product metric and is unrelated to data retention windows on platform infrastructure.

## Support

Support tickets fell 22% month on month after the succulent identification fix shipped in late June.
