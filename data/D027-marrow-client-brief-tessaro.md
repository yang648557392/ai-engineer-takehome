# Marrow & Co - Client Brief, Tessaro Kitchens

Tessaro Kitchens is an Italian kitchen manufacturer opening two showrooms in Belgium.

## Scope

- Launch campaign for the Antwerp and Ghent showrooms.
- Four week content sprint.
- Fee: EUR 31,000 fixed.

## Constraints

Tessaro has no restriction on generative image tools.
All copy must be delivered in Dutch and French.

## Contacts

Client side lead is the Tessaro Benelux country manager.
Marrow side lead is the Marrow account director.
