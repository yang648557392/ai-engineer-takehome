# Orlo - v2 Launch Retrospective

## What went well

The plant identification feature shipped on time.
Day one crash rate was 0.4%, below the 1% target.
App store rating held at 4.5.

## What went badly

Identification accuracy on succulents was poor and generated most of the support volume.
We had no offline evaluation set for identification, so we only learned this from user reports.
The team shipped without a golden set, which the group wiki says is required.

## Actions

- Build an identification golden set of at least 200 labelled photos. Owner Orlo ML. Open.
- Add a feedback button on every identification result. Owner Orlo mobile. Done.
