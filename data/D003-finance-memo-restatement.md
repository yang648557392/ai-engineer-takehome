# Finance Memo - Kettlebridge Q2 2027 ARR Restatement

Prepared by group finance.

## Summary

The ARR figure reported in the Q2 2027 Kettlebridge board deck was overstated.
Three annual contracts totalling EUR 140,000 were counted at signature date rather than at contract start date.
Two of those contracts start in Q3 2027 and one was cancelled before start.

**Restated ARR at end of Q2 2027: EUR 1,280,000.**

## Effect

The restatement does not change the Q2 net new logo count.
Gross margin is unchanged at 71%.
Per group policy this memo supersedes the figure in the board deck.
