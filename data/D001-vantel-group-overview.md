# Vantel Group - Portfolio Overview

Vantel Group is a venture holding with four operating companies sharing a central platform team.
The platform team runs shared infrastructure, procurement, and data services for all ventures.

## Ventures

- **Kettlebridge** - B2B freight operations SaaS. Founded 2023. Amsterdam.
- **Orlo** - consumer plant care mobile app. Founded 2024. Utrecht.
- **Pindrop Labs** - industrial vibration sensors and monitoring. Founded 2022. Eindhoven.
- **Marrow & Co** - brand and content agency, serves both external clients and the portfolio. Founded 2021. Rotterdam.

## Shared services

The platform team owns the shared Postgres cluster, the shared vector database cluster, the identity provider, and the internal agent gateway.
Ventures do not run their own production databases without a signed exception from the platform lead.

## Reporting

Each venture reports monthly numbers into the group finance pack.
Restatements are published as separate finance memos and supersede any number in an earlier board deck.
