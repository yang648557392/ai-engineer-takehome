# Marrow & Co - Client Brief, Halden Bikes

Halden Bikes is a Danish e-bike retailer expanding into the Netherlands.

## Scope

- Brand refresh for the Dutch market.
- Six week content sprint.
- Fee: EUR 48,000 fixed.

## Constraints

Halden supplies product photography.
Marrow must not use generative image tools for anything shipped to Halden, per the client contract clause 7.3.
Text drafting with LLM assistance is allowed if a human edits and signs off.

## Contacts

Client side lead is the Halden marketing director.
Marrow side lead is the Marrow account director.
